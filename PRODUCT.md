# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and team managers in a 5-person engineering team. They use this during the workday, switching between their editor and their task board. They move fast, care about precision, and expect tools to feel as intentional as the code they write. Two roles: Manager (creates tasks, assigns work, views cross-team metrics) and Employee (executes tasks, updates status, collaborates within their team).

## Product Purpose

Mini-Jira is a team-scoped task management platform on AWS. It exists to give small engineering teams a fast, opinionated workspace for tracking work without the overhead of enterprise tooling. Success means a manager can assign and track work across the team in under a minute, and an employee can move a task from To Do to Done without friction.

## Brand Personality

Professional, efficient, minimal. The tool gets out of your way. Every element earns its presence. The interface should feel like a considered piece of software, not a form wrapped in a skin.

## Anti-references

Boring, regular, generic. Not Jira's cluttered enterprise UI. Not generic SaaS dashboards with identical card grids and blue primary buttons. Not neon-on-black terminal aesthetics. Not glassmorphism used decoratively — only when it communicates depth.

## Design Principles

1. **Efficiency is the aesthetic.** Every visual decision should make the primary task faster, not just more beautiful.
2. **Depth without decoration.** Blur and layering are structural — they separate surfaces that need separating, not atmosphere for its own sake.
3. **One thing is primary.** Each screen has one primary action. Secondary options are subordinate, not hidden.
4. **Information at a glance.** Status, priority, and assignee should be readable before a user consciously focuses on a card.
5. **Bold, not loud.** The interface can make committed aesthetic choices without overwhelming the work being tracked.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Full keyboard navigation for Kanban (essential for power users). Status and priority must not rely on color alone — icon or label required alongside color. Reduced motion respected.
